# Index — src/components/map

- [[TrackMapCanvas.tsx]] — The cold canvas renderer (plan phase 2; phase 5 adds the side-by-side edge placement and the transition edge labels; phase 6 draws a cluster differently): genre-coloured discs, one line style per relationship type, the orange cut and the focus ring on the playing and selected tracks, hover highlighting its edges and dimming the rest. A cluster node (phase 6) draws hollow — a tinted arc of its genre colour with a hairline outline, sized by its track count and labelled with its value and count — so a group never reads as one track, and clicking one chooses that cluster. Edges that share a pair are drawn beside each other as a shallow bend whose ends stay on their nodes, so several relationship types — or A→B and B→A — never overlap (TM-L1, TM-L3), and a selected track's transition edges show their deduplicated notes as labels (TM-L2, TM-L6). Positions are pinned, so it never simulates and never redraws while idle; it reads the design tokens at runtime, scales the backing store by devicePixelRatio, draws text after `document.fonts.ready` and redraws on a theme or cover-mode change. `forwardRef` exposes `TrackMapCanvasHandle` (zoom in/out, fit, reset, centre on, zoom level).
- [[TrackDetails.tsx]] — The selected track's details: cover, title, artist, BPM, key, genre, ratings and tags (full variant); the partners and nearest tracks (each with its similarity as a whole percentage); the transitions in and out; and the actions Play (a run of one), Properties, Song page, Show its relationships and Open in map (the dialog's way onto the full map, phase 7, TM-E3).
- [[rail/]] — The map rail's sections (phase 3): one collapsible section per file, with the selected track's full details first; phase 4a adds Similarity, Filters and their collapse, phase 4b the core chip, phase 5 the Layers section with its legend and phase 6 the Clusters section and the cluster-filter chips.

The layout the canvas draws is solved headless in [[../../lib/trackMap/00_Index]] (`layout.ts`), and what it may draw under the active layers comes from `lib/trackMap/layerEdges.ts` (phase 5; phase 6 makes a clustered model's cluster links follow the nearest switch alone); the dialog uses the canvas for its graph tab, and `/map` (phase 3) uses it for the whole library and (phase 6) for its clusters. The canvas's one motion is the 400 ms re-layout (phase 4b), timed by `lib/trackMap/positionsAnimation.ts`. See `docs/plans/TRACK_MAP_PLAN.md` for the phases.
